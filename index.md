# Privacy Policy for JobFinder

**Effective Date:** October 23, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 23, 2026

JobFinder respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of users of the JobFinder mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information JobFinder may collect, how the information is used, how it is stored, and the choices available to users.

By using JobFinder, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. About JobFinder

JobFinder is a job search and recruitment application designed to connect job seekers and employers.

The application may provide features such as:

- Creating and managing user accounts
- Browsing available job opportunities
- Saving job listings
- Applying for jobs
- Managing job applications
- Creating and managing job seeker profiles or résumés
- Posting job vacancies
- Managing applicants
- Updating application statuses
- Administrative management of users, jobs, and applications

Users may be assigned roles such as:

- Job Seeker
- Employer
- Administrator

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use JobFinder, the application may collect the following information.

### Account Information

When you create an account, JobFinder may collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- User role
- Firebase user identification number (UID)

Passwords are handled through Firebase Authentication. JobFinder does not intentionally store users' passwords directly in its application database.

### Profile and Résumé Information

Job seekers may voluntarily provide information such as:

- Contact information
- Address or location
- Educational background
- Skills
- Work experience
- Career information
- Certifications
- Résumé details
- Other information included in a user profile

Users should avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information in their profiles or résumés.

### Job Application Information

When a job seeker applies for a position, JobFinder may process information such as:

- Applicant name
- Applicant email address
- Job applied for
- Company
- Application date
- Application status
- Résumé or profile information associated with the application

Application statuses may include statuses such as Pending, Under Review, Shortlisted, Accepted, Rejected, or Withdrawn.

### Employer Information

Employers may provide information such as:

- Company name
- Job title
- Job description
- Job location
- Salary information
- Employment type
- Job category
- Application deadline
- Applicant management information

### Saved Jobs

JobFinder may store information about jobs that users save or bookmark so that these jobs can be accessed later.

## 3. Firebase Services

JobFinder uses services provided by Google Firebase.

These services may include:

- Firebase Authentication
- Cloud Firestore

Firebase Authentication is used to manage account registration, login, password reset, and authentication.

Cloud Firestore is used to store application-related information such as user profiles, jobs, saved jobs, and job applications.

Firebase may process certain technical information in accordance with Google's own privacy policies and terms.

More information about Google's privacy practices is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. How We Use Information

Information collected through JobFinder may be used to:

- Create and manage user accounts
- Authenticate users
- Provide Job Seeker, Employer, and Administrator features
- Display available job opportunities
- Allow users to save job listings
- Allow job seekers to submit applications
- Allow employers to review applicants
- Update and display application statuses
- Maintain user profiles and résumé information
- Allow employers to create and manage job postings
- Maintain security and prevent unauthorized access
- Improve the functionality and reliability of JobFinder

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the operation of JobFinder unless users are informed and appropriate consent is obtained where required.

## 5. How Information Is Shared

JobFinder does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared when necessary to provide the application's functions.

For example, information submitted by a Job Seeker as part of an application may be made available to the Employer responsible for the relevant job posting.

Administrators may have access to certain account, job, and application information when necessary to operate, moderate, secure, or maintain the platform.

Information may also be processed by service providers such as Firebase that provide infrastructure necessary for the application to operate.

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled through JobFinder.

Authentication is provided through Firebase Authentication, and application data may be protected using Firebase security rules and access controls.

However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for:

- Keeping their login credentials confidential
- Using a strong password
- Avoiding sharing their account with unauthorized individuals
- Protecting access to their devices

## 7. Data Retention

Personal information may be retained while a user's account remains active or while the information is necessary to provide JobFinder services.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups or technical systems after deletion.

Information may also be retained when required for legitimate security, legal, fraud-prevention, or administrative purposes.

If personal information from a child is discovered to have been collected without appropriate authorization, reasonable steps will be taken to remove it.
