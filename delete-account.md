# Delete Your JobFinder Account

JobFinder users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

## How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your JobFinder account, send an email to:

**[renanudon@gmail.com]**

Use the subject:

**JobFinder Account Deletion Request**

Please include:

- Your full name
- The email address registered with your JobFinder account
- A clear statement that you want your JobFinder account deleted

## What Will Be Deleted

After your request is verified, JobFinder may delete or remove:

- Your JobFinder user account
- Profile information
- Résumé or career information stored in the app
- Saved jobs
- Job applications associated with your account
- Other personal information linked to your JobFinder account

Some information may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or legitimate administrative purposes.

## Verification

For security reasons, we may ask you to verify that you are the owner of the account before deletion is completed.

## Contact

If you have questions about account deletion or privacy, contact:

**[renanudon@gmail.com]**
